Dear Editor,

\vspace{6pt}
I wish to submit the article ``Every Algebraic Number is a Critical Value of an
Integer Polynomial'' for consideration by The American Mathematical Monthly.
This work is original and has not been published elsewhere, nor is it currently
under consideration for publication elsewhere. A preprint is posted on GitHub.

\vspace{6pt}

The main theorem of this paper is in the title, and the construction is
explicit. This answers a 2017 question of Schwarz, Vologodsky and Walcher.
Petrov arrived at an equivalent construction, without knowing that the
question was open. I arrived at Petrov's theorem by reducing the question to
its statement. The main new ingredient compared with Petrov's argument is
arranging integer coefficients, while preserving the prescribed critical value.

\vspace{6pt}

I also classify the integer polynomials that can have a given algebraic number
as a critical value, and give a lower bound on their degree. A companion
website provides an online calculator.

\vspace{6pt}

Since this article is short, Professor Dingxing Zhang told me the American
Mathematical Monthly would be suitable for it.

\vspace{6pt}

Keywords: critical values, integer polynomials, algebraic numbers, Petrov's
theorem, Lean formalization.
