Title
  Every Algebraic Number is a Critical Value of an Integer Polynomial

Abstract (plain text version of the manuscript's abstract)
  We arrived at an independent proof of Petrov's theorem. For every
  irreducible f ∈ ℤ[x] of positive degree there are g ∈ ℤ[x] and an
  irreducible F ∈ ℤ[x] of positive degree with F | g′ and F² | f∘g.
  Equivalently, every algebraic number is a critical value of an integer
  polynomial, so the set of critical values of ℤ[x] is exactly ℚ̄. The
  construction is explicit. We classify g ∈ ℤ[x] with critical value α, and
  find a lower bound for deg g. The proof was assisted by AI, and has been
  formalized in Lean.
